Last updated: October 21, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use Genjutsu Trends, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Israeli standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we've done

  • The whole site works with a keyboard, including the photo and video upload boxes, with a visible focus outline.
  • Form fields have labels, buttons have text or accessible names, and status messages are announced.
  • Text and buttons use high-contrast colors, and pages adapt to phone screens and browser zoom without horizontal scrolling.
  • Sign-in uses a 6-digit code typed on the same page, so you don't have to switch between apps.

Known limitations

  • Trend example videos are short music clips without captions or audio description.
  • Videos play muted on loop by default; each has a button to turn sound on.
  • The PayPal checkout window is provided by PayPal, and its accessibility is outside our control.

Contact us

If something on the site is hard to use, or you need help making a video, email our accessibility contact at support@genjutsu-trends.com. Please describe the page and the problem, and we'll reply as soon as we can.

הצהרת נגישות (בקצרה)

אנו פועלים להנגיש את האתר לפי התקן הישראלי ת"י 5568 (WCAG 2.1 ברמה AA). האתר ניתן לתפעול מלא במקלדת, שדות הטפסים מתויגים והצבעים בניגודיות גבוהה. מגבלות ידועות: סרטוני הדוגמה הם קטעי מוזיקה קצרים ללא כתוביות, וחלון התשלום של PayPal אינו בשליטתנו. נתקלתם בבעיה? כתבו לנו: support@genjutsu-trends.com.
