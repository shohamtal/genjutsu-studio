Genjutsu is Higgsfield's video-to-video model. Give it a clip and one or more photos, and it recasts the clip with your people, objects or locations while keeping the original motion, camera move and timing. Genjutsu Trends is an independent site that makes trend videos with it: pick a scene, add photos, pay per video.

Genjutsu Trends is not affiliated with or endorsed by Higgsfield AI. Videos are made with Higgsfield's Genjutsu model through its official API. "Higgsfield" and "Genjutsu" are used here only to describe the model behind the videos.

Limits

  • Source video: MP4, 4–30 seconds. Output length matches the source.
  • Reference images: 1–8 (JPG, PNG, WEBP).
  • Resolution: 480p (default), 720p or 1080p.
  • Optional text instructions, e.g. "Replace both characters with the uploaded pictures".

FAQ

What is Higgsfield Genjutsu? Genjutsu is Higgsfield's video-to-video model, announced on August 31, 2026. It changes a video without reshooting it: it keeps the motion, camera work and timing and swaps the cast, objects or look using reference images.

Is Genjutsu Trends part of Higgsfield? No. Genjutsu Trends is an independent site, not affiliated with or endorsed by Higgsfield AI. It makes videos with the Genjutsu model through Higgsfield's official API, and you pay per video, with no plan to sign up for.

What's the difference between Motion Transfer and Object Swap? Motion Transfer keeps the movement and rebuilds the scene or characters from your images. Object Swap replaces one element, such as a person, outfit or product, and keeps the rest of the footage intact.

What are Genjutsu's limits? The source video must be 4 to 30 seconds long. You can add 1 to 8 reference images, and output goes up to 1080p.

How much does a video cost? On Genjutsu Trends, 480p is 47.7 credits per second, 720p is 102.15 and 1080p is 244.8, plus 100 credits per video (100 credits = $1). A 10-second 720p clip is about $11.22.
