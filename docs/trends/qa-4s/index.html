Photo tips

  • One person per photo, facing the camera.
  • Face and upper body in frame, not too far away.
  • Good, even light: daylight by a window works well.
  • No sunglasses, masks, heavy filters or group shots.
  • One photo per part (2 parts), in the order shown on the page.

Questions

Is this public?

No. It is hidden from the gallery, the sitemap and search engines.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Everything runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer. Sign in with your email (we send a 6-digit code) and you're ready.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes. You can close the tab: the video keeps rendering, it appears under "My videos" and we email you when it's ready.

Which resolution should I pick?

480p is the default and looks fine on TikTok, Reels and Shorts. 720p and 1080p are sharper and cost more; the price updates as you switch.

What if the video doesn't come out well?

If the render fails or is blocked by the automatic content check, your credits are refunded automatically. A finished video can't be refunded, because the render has already been paid for, so use a clear, well-lit photo for the best result.

Will the video follow my prompt exactly?

Not always. The video is made by an AI model, and like every AI video model today it can miss or change details of a prompt, or keep details from the original scene, such as clothing or accessories. The outfit and the setting come from the trend's scene. Some ideas take more than one try, so start at 480p to see how it looks.

Can I download and share it?

Yes. Download the MP4 from "My videos" and post it anywhere, or share a link to it.
