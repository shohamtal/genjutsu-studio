About the trend

The joke is the contrast: a heavy 4x4 launches over a dune in a cloud of golden dust and lands a few meters away, while the man on the chair doesn't move a muscle. This version puts you on that chair.

Genjutsu keeps the original motion, camera moves and timing and rebuilds the person from your selfie.

How to make it

  1. Open this page. The Desert Truck Jump clip and the prompt are already loaded. You don't need to find the video or a CapCut template.
  2. Add one selfie. A clear, front-facing, well-lit photo of the person who'll take the part.
  3. Keep 480p. 480p is the default: it's the cheapest and looks fine on TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Pick 720p or 1080p for a sharper video.
  4. Click Generate. You'll see the exact price first. The video renders in a few minutes, and you can close the tab and come back.
  5. Download and post. Download the MP4 and post it with the trend's sound.

Photo tips

  • One person per photo, facing the camera.
  • Face and upper body in frame, not too far away.
  • Good, even light: daylight by a window works well.
  • No sunglasses, masks, heavy filters or group shots.
  • Only use photos of people who agreed to be in the video.
