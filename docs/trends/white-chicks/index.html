About the trend

In the 2004 comedy White Chicks, Terry Crews' character Latrell sings Vanessa Carlton's "A Thousand Miles" in his car, and the scene has been a meme for twenty years. In 2026 AI face swaps made it blow up again, peaking during the World Cup: people swap in themselves, their friends or their favorite players and post it to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Most face swap apps paste a flat face over the clip. Genjutsu works differently. It keeps the original motion, camera move and timing and rebuilds the characters from your photos, so the car lighting and every head turn match.

How to make it

  1. Open this page. The White Chicks car scene and the prompt are already loaded. You don't need to find the clip or a CapCut template.
  2. Add two photos. Add one photo for the person on the left (passenger seat) and one for the person on the right (Terry Crews' seat, the driver). Use clear, front-facing, well-lit photos with one face each.
  3. Keep 480p. 480p is the default: it's the cheapest and looks fine on TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Pick 720p or 1080p for a sharper video.
  4. Click Generate. You'll see the exact price first. The video renders in a few minutes, and you can close the tab and come back.
  5. Download and post. Download the MP4 and post it. Add "A Thousand Miles" in the app you post with for the full meme.

Photo tips

  • One person per photo, facing the camera.
  • Face and upper body in frame, not too far away.
  • Good, even light: daylight by a window works well.
  • No sunglasses, masks, heavy filters or group shots.
  • One photo per part (2 parts), in the order shown on the page.
  • Only use photos of people who agreed to be in the video.
