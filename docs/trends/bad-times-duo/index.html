About the trend

The "Bad Times" trend is all about dramatic arguing gestures: hands on the head, a finger wag, a palm pushed right into the camera. This version does it as a pair, in sharp suits in front of a wooden fence.

Genjutsu keeps the original motion, camera moves and timing and rebuilds both people from your photos.

How to make it

Photo tips

  • One person per photo, facing the camera.
  • Face and upper body in frame, not too far away.
  • Good, even light: daylight by a window works well.
  • No sunglasses, masks, heavy filters or group shots.
  • One photo per part (2 parts), in the order shown on the page.
  • Only use photos of people who agreed to be in the video.
