How to make it

  1. Open this page. The Shrek clip and the prompt are already loaded.

Photo tips

  • One person per photo, facing the camera.
  • Face and upper body in frame, not too far away.
  • Good, even light: daylight by a window works well.
  • No sunglasses, masks, heavy filters or group shots.
  • One photo per part (2 parts), in the order shown on the page.
  • Only use photos of people who agreed to be in the video.
