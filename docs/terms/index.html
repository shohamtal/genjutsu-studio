2. Your account

You sign in with your email address. Keep access to that email private: anyone who can read it can sign in as you. You must be at least 18 to buy credits.

3. Credits, prices and payments

  • 100 credits = US$1. Credits don't expire, can't be transferred and have no cash value.
  • The price of each video is shown before you click Generate and depends on the clip length and resolution. Credits are taken when the video starts.
  • If a finished video runs longer than the seconds it was billed for, the extra seconds are charged at the same rate, even if that takes your balance below zero.
  • Payments are processed by PayPal. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Prices may change. A change never affects a video you already started.

4. Refunds

  • If a video fails or is blocked by moderation, its credits go back to your balance automatically. In the rare case we need to check a video by hand, this happens within a day.
  • Unused credits can be refunded to your original payment method if you ask within 14 days of buying them. Email us from the address you signed in with.
  • Credits spent on a video that finished can't be refunded, because the generation cost has already been paid.
  • If a payment is refunded or charged back, the matching credits are removed, and the account may be paused while a dispute is open.

The price you see before you click Generate is for the attempt, not for a specific result (a render that fails or is blocked is still refunded, as described in section 4). Look at the trend's scene and its example before you buy, and start at a low resolution if you're unsure.
